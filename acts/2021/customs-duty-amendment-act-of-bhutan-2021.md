# Customs Duty Amendment Act of Bhutan 2021

Preamble

An Act to amend the BTC code, Commodity Description and propose duty rate for goods classified under Schedule 1 of the Customs Duty Act of Bhutan 2021;

Parliament of the Kingdom of Bhutan do hereby enacts as follows:

### CHAPTER 1

PRELIMINARY

**Title**

#### 1.

1. This Act is the Customs Duty (Amendment) Act of Bhutan 2021.

**Commencement**

#### 2.

2. This Act comes into force on the day the Act is introduced by the Finance Minister in the National Assembly of Bhutan on the 21st Day of the 10th Month of the Female Iron Ox Year corresponding to the 25th Day of November 2021.

#### 3.

3. In the Customs Duty Act of Bhutan 2021, hereinafter referred to as the Act, following amendment to Schedule 1 is amended as:
