Compilation of GST Bill 2021.indd 6 7/15/21 3:56 PM Preamble

An Act to amend Goods and Services Tax Act of Bhutan 2020.

Parliament of the Kingdom of Bhutan hereby enacts as follows:

**Title**

#### 1.

1. This Act is the Goods and Services Tax (Amendment) Act of Bhutan 2021.

**Commencement**

#### 2.

2. This Act comes into force on the day the Act is introduced by the Finance Minister in the National Assembly of Bhutan on 20th Day of the 4th Month of the Female Iron Ox Year corresponding to the 31st Day of May 2021.

**Extent**

#### 3.

3. This Act extends to the whole of the kingdom of Bhutan.
