The Pay Revision (Amendment) Act of Bhutan 2020 |i PREAMBLE

An Act to amend certain provisions of the Pay Revision Act of Bhutan 2019. Parliament of the Kingdom of Bhutan do hereby enact as follows:

**Title**

#### 1.

1. This Act is the Pay Revision (Amendment) Act of Bhutan 2020.

**Commencement**

#### 2.

2. This Act comes into force retroactively from 1st July 2019.

**Extent**

#### 3.

3. This Act extends to the whole of the Kingdom of Bhutan.

#### 6.

6. In the Act, Section 32 is amended as:

Discretionary Grant “Discretionary Grant shall be as follows: (1) The discretionary grant for the Members of Parliament shall be revised from Nu.0.100 m to Nu.0.150m per annum.

The Pay Revision (Amendment) Act of Bhutan 2020 |3 (2) The discretionary grant for Dzongdags shall be revised from Nu.0.050 m to Nu.0.075 m per annum. (3) The discretionary grant for other public servants shall be maintained at the existing level. The term Discretionary Allowance in Annexure 2, 3, 4, 5 & 7 shall read as Discretionary Grant”.

#### 7.

7. In the Act, Section 56 is amended as:

Children Education Allowance (CEA) “The children education allowance is provided to cover the cost of education during foreign posting. The CEA shall compensate for rising cost of education in the respective country. For children studying outside Bhutan, the CEA allowance of the place of study shall be applicable (for e.g. If a parent is working in Geneva and children are studying in India, New Delhi rate shall be applicable, similarly if a parent is working in New Delhi and children are studying in New York , New Delhi rate shall be applied). For the countries/places not listed in the children education allowance, the rate of the place of posting shall apply. For Children studying inside Bhutan, the South Asia rate shall apply”.
