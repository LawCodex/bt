Parliament of the Kingdom of Bhutan hereby enact the Penal Code (Amendment) Act of Bhutan 2011 on the 22nd Day of the 3rd Month of Iron Female Rabbit Year of the Bhutanese Calendar corresponding to the 24th Day of May, 2011 at its 7th Session of the First Parliament as follows:

**Short Title, Commencement and Extent**

#### 1.

1. This Act shall:

a) Be called PENAL CODE (AMENDMENT)

ACT OF BHUTAN 2011;

b) Come into force on the 4th Day of the 5th Month of Iron Female Rabbit Year of the Bhutanese Calendar corresponding to the 5th Day of July, 2011; and c) Extend to the whole of Bhutan.
