# The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015

Preamble

Recognizing that the Constitution of the Kingdom of Bhutan establishes the Office of the Attorney General as an autonomous institution to carry out the responsibilities within the domain and authority of the Government and such other legal matters as may be entrusted;

With due reverence to the noble vision of the Monarchs to promote and ensure justice through fair, impartial and just proceedings before the Courts and tribunals to uphold rule of law in the society with responsive and accessible legal process; and

Recognizing the importance of having an autonomous institution to provide professional legal advice, draft laws and represent the Government before the Courts;

Parliament of the Kingdom of Bhutan do hereby enact the Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 on the 25th Day of the 3rd Month of Wood Female Sheep Year of the Bhutanese Calendar corresponding to the 13th Day of May 2015 as follows:

The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 1

### CHAPTER I

PRELIMINARY

**Title, commencement and extent**

#### 1.

1. This Act shall:

#### 1.

1. Be called the Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015;

#### 2.

2. Come into force on the 4th day of the 6th month of the Wood Female Sheep Year of the Bhutanese calendar, corresponding to the 20th day of July 2015; and

#### 3.

3. Extend to the whole of the Kingdom of Bhutan.

**Repeal**

#### 2.

2. This Act shall repeal the Office of the Attorney General Act 2006 and manuals, guidelines, and directives issued there under.

**Application**

#### 3.

3. This Act shall govern the powers and functions of the employees of the Office of the Attorney General.

### CHAPTER II

OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

**Establishment of the Office of the Attorney General**

#### 4.

4. There shall be an Office of the Attorney General, which shall be autonomous to carry out the responsibilities within the domain and authority of the Government and such other legal matters as may be entrusted to the Office by the Government.

#### 5.

5. The Office shall provide professional legal services to the Government.

#### 6.

6. The Office shall be headed by the Attorney General and shall be assisted by a Deputy Attorney General.

#### 7.

7. The Office may establish departments, divisions and sections as deemed necessary by the Attorney General in consultation with the Royal Civil Service Commission.

#### 8.

8. The Government shall provide adequate facilities and funds for the smooth functioning of the Office.

#### 9.

9. Except for the Attorney General, the service conditions of the employees of the Office shall be governed by the Civil Service Act.

### CHAPTER III

POWERS AND FUNCTIONS OF THE OFFICE

**Litigation and prosecution**

#### 10.

10. The Office shall endeavor to promote fair, impartial and just proceedings in civil and criminal cases.

#### 11.

11. The Office shall in accordance with the Article 29 of the Constitution of Kingdom of Bhutan be the central litigation and prosecuting agency of the Government.

#### 12.

12. The Office shall represent the Government in a civil litigation and law suits filed against the Government before the Courts or tribunals.

#### 13.

13. In a civil litigation the Office may:

#### 1.

1. Represent the Government before the Courts;

#### 2.

2. Seek declarations, orders, directions or writs as may be appropriate in the circumstances of each case; and

#### 3.

3. Seek appropriate remedy from the Court when a person or public servant refuses to comply with the lawful orders of the authority.

#### 14.

14. The Office shall not represent any private citizen in a civil litigation.

**The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 4**

#### 15.

15. Notwithstanding anything contained in section 14 of this Act, the Office may seek civil remedies that directly relates to the criminal prosecution.

#### 16.

16. The Office shall prosecute criminal cases in accordance with the provisions of this Act when referred to it by any agency of the Government under the relevant laws.

#### 17.

17. The Office shall:

#### 1.

1. Advise the Government on orders passed by Courts in and outside Bhutan as and when required; and

#### 2.

2. Render necessary advice and support to the Government for the execution of judgments when required.

#### 18.

18. The Office may obtain relevant information, particulars and necessary documents pertaining to a case and the Government, concerned agency or relevant entity shall not withhold information and documentary evidence.

#### 19.

19. The Office may call for presence of an official or employee of the Government or relevant agency at any stage of proceedings before the Courts as may be required during the prosecution or a civil litigation.

#### 20.

20. The Office may, by issuing a power of attorney, delegate the prosecution of a particular case to legal personnel of any agency of government or other institution by issuing a power of attorney who shall act in accordance with this Act. Provided that this does not prejudice the powers of prosecution granted to any other agency through their respective Acts.

#### 21.

21. The power of attorney may be issued on the condition that the prosecution shall be carried out under the supervision and monitoring of the Office.

#### 22.

22. The power of attorney issued under sections 20 and 21 of this Act may be revoked and the case shall be taken over by the Office at any stage of the case if:

#### 1.

1. Such legal personnel brings to the notice of the Office that he or she is unable to perform the job fairly and justly due to interference or undue influence of any individual in the agency or organization; or

#### 2.

2. The Office has reason to believe that the legal personnel may not perform the job fairly and justly due to his or her conflict of interest in a case.

#### 23.

23. When the case proceedings are ongoing before the Courts, if the Office deems it necessary to seek additional information

**Referral of case**

#### 24.

24. Any agency of the Government may refer a case for litigation or prosecution and shall provide the Office with a complete report of the case and evidence.

**Review of the case**

#### 25.

25. Upon receipt of the case for litigation or prosecution, the Office shall expeditiously review facts of the case.

#### 26.

26. If the Office deems that additional facts are necessary, the referring agency shall be asked to provide the facts.

#### 27.

27. Upon ascertainment of the facts of the case, the Office shall determine whether the act committed by the person constitutes an offence under the law.

#### 28.

28. If the case referred is of civil nature, the Office shall determine the law under which a relief can be sought before the Court.

**The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 7**

#### 29.

29. In criminal cases, once an act is determined to be an offence under the law, the Office shall determine whether to initiate prosecution by applying a test for prosecution.

**Initiation of prosecution**

#### 30.

30. The Office shall initiate prosecution only if both the evidential and public interest test are fulfilled.

#### 31.

31. The test for prosecution shall be deemed to have been fulfilled if in the opinion of the Office:

#### 1.

1. There is sufficient credible evidence to prove a case beyond reasonable doubt; and

#### 2.

2. The prosecution of a case is required in the public interest.

#### 32.

32. In applying a test for prosecution, the Office shall comply with the provisions of this Act and any other laws in force.

#### 33.

33. In a civil case, preponderance of evidence shall be the test for initiation of litigation. In the case of a constitutional issue, no such test shall be applicable.

The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 8 Evidential test 34. The evidential test shall require that every element of an offence is supported by the corresponding evidence.

#### 35.

35. The Office shall determine whether there is sufficient evidence to prove a case beyond reasonable doubt.

#### 36.

36. The Office shall consider only that evidence which is available and admissible before the Court of law.

#### 37.

37. The Office shall initiate prosecution only if the evidence sufficiently identifies a person or persons or institutions responsible for the crime.

#### 38.

38. The Office shall not initiate prosecution if there are concerns on the credibility of evidence.

**Public interest test**

#### 39.

39. Once the evidential test is fulfilled, the Office shall apply the public interest test to decide whether to initiate prosecution of an offence.

The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 9 Public interest considerations for prosecution 40. The public interest considerations for prosecution shall include cases where:

#### 1.

1. A conviction is likely to result in a significant sentence;

#### 2.

2. The defendant was in a position of authority or trust and the offence is in abuse of that position;

#### 3.

3. The offence is committed against a person serving the public;

#### 4.

4. The defendant is an organizer of the offence;

#### 5.

5. The offence is premeditated;

#### 6.

6. The offence is carried out by a group;

#### 7.

7. The offence is motivated by hostility against a person because of his or her race, ethnicity, sexual orientation, disability, religion, political belief, age or the like;

#### 8.

8. The offence has resulted in substantial financial loss to an individual, corporate person or society;

#### 9.

9. The victim of the offence or his or her family has been put in fear or suffered personal attack, damage or disturbance;

#### 10.

10. There is a marked difference between the ages of the defendant and the victim and the defendant took advantage of this;

#### 11.

11. The defendant’s previous conviction is relevant to the present offence; or

#### 12.

12. There are grounds for believing that the offence is likely to continue or be repeated.

**Public interest consideration against prosecution**

#### 41.

41. The public interest considerations against prosecution shall include:

#### 1.

1. Likelihood of imposing a very small or nominal penalty;

#### 2.

2. The loss or harm can be described as minor and is the result of a single incident, particularly if it is caused by an error of judgment or a genuine mistake;

#### 3.

3. The offence is not of a serious nature and is unlikely to be repeated;

#### 4.

4. That a prosecution is likely to have an adverse effect on the victim’s physical or mental health, bearing in mind the seriousness of the offence;

#### 5.

5. That the defendant is elderly or is or was at the time of commission of the offence, suffering from significant mental or physical ill health, unless the offence is serious or there is real possibility that it may be repeated; or

#### 6.

6. Where the offence is described as a minor and the defendant has put right the loss or harm.

#### 1.

1. There is insufficient evidence; or

#### 2.

2. There is overriding public interest against the prosecution or litigation.

#### 43.

43. Where the office is of the opinion that the case is of administrative nature, the case may be returned to the referring agency.

**Timeframe for case review**

#### 44.

44. The Office may enter into agreement with the investigating or referring agency and agree on the case referral procedure including sharing of additional information and evidence, and the time frame within which decision shall be made by the Office.

**Conflict of interest**

#### 45.

45. The Office shall not assign case to an employee who may have or be reasonably construed to have conflict of interests on the case at hand.

The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 12 Plea bargain 46. The Office may consider plea bargain which shall be in conformity with the Civil and Criminal Procedure Code of Bhutan.

**Withdrawal of prosecution**

#### 47.

47. During the pendency of the prosecution, the Office may withdraw the charge if:

#### 1.

1. The accused dies; or

#### 2.

2. New evidence proving innocence of the accused is produced.

#### 48.

48. Notwithstanding anything contained in section 47 of this Act, the accused shall not be absolved of the civil liability associated with the charge.

#### 49.

49. The Office may withdraw a civil litigation in accordance with the provisions of the Civil and Criminal Procedure Code.

**Office not to defend a suspect**

#### 50.

50. The Office shall not defend any public servant who has been charged of a criminal offence, including corruption charges, before any Court of law. The role of the Office shall be limited to defending public servants whose bonafide official decisions have been challenged in a Court of law.

The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 13 Legal services 51. The Office:

#### 1.

1. Shall provide legal services and opinions on matters of law and such other issues assigned or referred to it by the Government;

#### 2.

2. May provide legal education to legal personnel; and

#### 3.

3. May disseminate information to enhance awareness of laws among the people.

#### 52.

52. The Government shall seek opinion from the Office in writing, through a concise statement on the subject, supported by documentation or other materials that may be necessary.

#### 53.

53. The Office shall provide legal opinion only upon the written directives of the Lhengye Zhungtshog.

**Drafting**

#### 54.

54. The Office shall draft Bills or delegated legislation referred to it by the Government.

#### 55.

55. The Government shall furnish clear and detailed policy guidelines including all the relevant documents to the Office to draft Bills.

**The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 14**

#### 56.

56. The Office shall translate policies into legal text upon receipt of the detailed policy guidelines from the Government.

#### 57.

57. The Ministry or relevant agency shall designate in writing an official to facilitate the Office to draft a Bill.

#### 58.

58. If the Government so desires, the Office shall discuss and consult the Bills with the relevant stakeholders.

#### 59.

59. The office may develop a drafting manual in consultation with relevant stakeholders.

**Review**

#### 60.

60. The Office:

#### 1.

1. Shall review Bills or delegated legislations, as may be referred to it by the Government;

#### 2.

2. May review laws and propose for necessary amendments to the Government;

#### 3.

3. May constitute committees to review laws as and when required; and

#### 4.

4. Shall endeavor to ensure that there is no duplicity or contradiction of laws by reviewing the form and content of Bills or delegated legislation.

### CHAPTER IV

ATTORNEY GENERAL

**The Attorney General**

#### 61.

61. The Druk Gyalpo shall, by warrant under His hand and seal, appoint an eminent jurist as the Attorney General on the recommendation of the Prime Minister.

#### 62.

62. A person eligible for appointment as the Attorney General shall:

#### 1.

1. Be a natural born citizen of Bhutan;

#### 2.

2. Be not married to a person who is not a citizen of Bhutan;

#### 3.

3. Be from among eminent jurists

#### 4.

4. Possess a LLB Degree from an institution of repute recognized by the Government;

#### 5.

5. Has served in the government, public or private service for a minimum of 15 years;

#### 6.

6. Not have political affiliations;

#### 7.

7. Not have been convicted of criminal offence under any law of the country and sentenced to imprisonment;

#### 8.

8. Not be in arrears of taxes or other dues to the Government;

#### 9.

9. Not have been terminated or compulsorily retired from the Government or public service on disciplinary grounds.

#### 63.

63. The Attorney General shall be accountable to the Druk Gyalpo and to the Prime Minister. The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 16 64. The term of the office of the Attorney General shall be five years or until attaining the age of sixty-five years, whichever is earlier.

#### 65.

65. The Attorney General may be eligible for reappointment, subject to a maximum of two terms.

#### 66.

66. The Attorney General shall be paid salary and benefits equivalent to Justices of the Supreme Court, provided that the salary and benefits shall not be varied to his or her disadvantage.

**Duties and responsibilities of the Attorney General**

#### 67.

67. The Attorney General, as the Chief Legal Officer, shall:

#### 1.

1. Defend the cause of the Kingdom and discharge his or her functions in pursuit of justice;

#### 2.

2. Advise the Government on all legal matters and perform such other duties of a legal nature as may be assigned or referred to him or her;

#### 3.

3. Have the right of audience in all Courts;

#### 4.

4. Administer the functioning of the Office; and

#### 5.

5. Ensure upholding of rule of law and justice.

**The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 17**

#### 68.

68. The Attorney General shall represent the Government in any Court outside the Kingdom of Bhutan, when necessary.

#### 69.

69. The Attorney General shall submit to the Druk Gyalpo and the Government such information and advice as may be required on legal matters or issues related to national interests.

#### 70.

70. The Attorney General shall have the power to institute, initiate or withdraw any case in accordance with the law.

#### 71.

71. The Attorney General may avail the services of qualified Jabmis, as and when deemed necessary, to perform specific legal assignments.

#### 72.

72. The Attorney General shall furnish opinion only in writing and oral communication shall not be considered as part of the opinion.

#### 73.

73. The Attorney General shall have the right to appear and express opinions on any legal question in Parliament.

#### 74.

74. The Attorney General shall:

#### 1.

1. Authorize and supervise Government prosecutors, special counsels or Jabmis for and on behalf of the Government, in any Court;

**The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 18**

#### 2.

2. Supervise formulation of plans and policies of the Office; and

#### 3.

3. Take any other action necessary in the administrative interests of the Office.

#### 75.

75. The Attorney General shall submit annual report on the activities of the Office to the Druk Gyalpo and to the Prime Minister.

**Accessibility**

#### 76.

76. The Attorney General shall have access to persons, relevant records, documents and properties pertaining to a civil or criminal case in the performance of his or her duties.

### CHAPTER V

CODE OF CONDUCT OF ATTORNEY GENERAL

**Loyalty and dedication**

#### 77.

77. The Attorney General shall perform his or her duties with complete loyalty and dedication to the Tsa-Wa-Sum and he or she shall not indulge in any activity that may affect the sovereignty and interests of the Kingdom of Bhutan.

**Responsibility**

#### 78.

78. The Attorney General shall ensure that his or her personal conduct is consistent with the dignity, image and integrity of the Office.

The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 19 Honesty 79. The Attorney General shall act honestly and with propriety in the performance of his or her duties and functions.

**Fairness and diligence**

#### 80.

80. The Attorney General shall not make an official decision without first giving due consideration to the matter at hand and the impact it is likely to have on the rights and interests of the people involved.

**Confidentiality**

#### 81.

81. The Attorney General shall maintain confidentiality in cases where the decisions and documents of the Office are likely to affect the interests of the nation adversely.

**Integrity**

#### 82.

82. The Attorney General shall not let himself/herself be influenced in any manner by any individual or any interest group in the discharge of his or her official duties.

**Selflessness**

#### 83.

83. The Attorney General shall take decisions solely in the interest of the nation and in the interest of justice and not on any other factors.

The Office of the Attorney General Act of Bhutan 2015 20 Transparency 84. The decisions and actions of the Attorney General shall be made transparent and he or she shall provide reasons for his or her decisions.

**Leadership**

#### 85.

85. The Attorney General shall display high moral values and conduct himself or herself at all times in a manner that reflects the credibility of the Office. He or she shall abide by the spirit and letter of laws and rules, and adhere to the ethical standards prescribed in this Act.

**Personal conduct**

#### 86.

86. The Attorney General shall comply with and execute laws and uphold the administration of justice.

#### 87.

87. The Attorney General entrusted with the sacred responsibility to serve the Tsa-Wa-Sum, shall:

#### 1.

1. Refrain from indulging in habits and behaviour that infringe upon the performance of official duties or tarnish the image of the Office;

#### 2.

2. Maintain dignity and decorum of the Office;

#### 3.

3. Not use Government resources for personal purposes and benefits;

#### 4.

4. Be liable for unlawful and improper behaviour or the non-performance of his or her duties;

#### 5.

5. Not do or direct to be done, in abuse of his or her office or power, any act prejudicial to the rights of another person knowing that such act is unlawful or contrary to Government policy;

#### 6.

6. Maintain transparency in the policies of the Office and in his or her decisions and actions;

#### 7.

7. Not mislead the Government knowingly on any matter of significance arising from his or her functions;

#### 8.

8. Not provide information which is detrimental to the interest, territorial integrity and sovereignty of the Kingdom; and

#### 9.

9. Protect and uphold professionalism of a Jabmi, render decisions based on merit, and shall prohibit all forms of discrimination.

**Conflict of interest**

#### 88.

88. The Attorney General shall ensure that no conflict of interest arises, or appears to arise between his or her official duties and his or her private interests, financial or otherwise. A conflict of interest may exist when the Attorney General is influenced or appears to be influenced by private interests.

#### 89.

89. The Attorney General shall:

#### 1.

1. Declare his or her income, assets and liabilities in accordance with the laws;

#### 2.

2. Not undertake any private trade, commercial activity or additional employment;

#### 3.

3. Not appear, advise or represent any party against the Government; and

#### 4.

4. Not be a member of, belong to, or take part in any society the membership of which is incompatible with the functions or dignity of his or her office.

### CHAPTER VII

MISCELLANEOUS

**Immunity from prosecution or civil Suit**

#### 98.

98. No criminal proceeding or civil suit shall be brought against any employee of the Office in respect to any proceeding in a Court, which has been done in good faith, in the course of his or her duties in accordance with the laws.

#### 99.

99. Such immunity shall not cover corrupt acts committed by any employee in connection with the discharge of his or her official duties.

**Savings**

#### 100.

100. This Act shall not affect the validity of any action or decision taken under the Office of the Attorney Generals Act 2006.
