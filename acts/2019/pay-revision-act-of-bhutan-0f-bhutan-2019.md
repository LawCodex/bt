# PAY REVISION Act OF BHUTAN 0F BHUTAN, 2019

Preamble

Whereas, in keeping with Article 30 of the Constitution of the Kingdom of Bhutan, the Fourth Pay Commission (4PC) was establish to recommend to the Government revisions in the structure of the salary, allowances, benefits, and other emoluments of the Public Servants;

Whereas, the 4PC submitted its recommendation for revision in the structure of the salary, allowances, benefits, and other emoluments of the Public Servants to the Lhengye Zhungtshog on 5th April 2019;

Whereas, Article 30 (3) of the Constitution states that the recommendations of the Commission shall be implemented only on the approval of the Lhengye Zhungtshog and subject to such conditions and modifications as may be made by Parliament.

Whereas, the Lhengye Zhungtshog approved the recommendation of the 4PC for implementation as provided in the Bill:

Parliament of the Kingdom of Bhutan hereby enacts as follows:

PAY REVISION Act OF BHUTAN 0F BHUTAN, 2019 1

### Chapter I

Preliminary

**Short Title**

#### 1.

1. This Act is the Pay Revision Act of Bhutan 2019.

**Commencement**

#### 2.

2. This Act comes into force on the 29th Day of the 4th Month of the Earth Female Pig Year of the Bhutanese Calendar corresponding to the 1st Day of July 2019.

**Extent**

#### 3.

3. This Act extends to the whole of the Kingdom of Bhutan.

**Scope**

#### 4.

4. This Act applies to:

1) A ‘Public Servant’ whose salary, allowances, benefits and other emoluments are drawn from the Consolidated Fund;

2) However, capitation fees, stipends, subsidies, and transfers drawn from the Consolidated Fund for salaries, allowances, benefits, and other emoluments for a ‘Public Servant’ are excluded. The revision shall be in accordance with the existing practice and by the Government as and when necessary.

**Repeal**

#### 5.

5. This Act repeals any provision of the Pay Revision Acts, Notifications, Circulars, Rules and Regulations on pay revision that are inconsistent with the provisions of this Bill.

#### 19.

19. There shall be maximum salary indexation of 5% or inflation whichever is lower. ### Chapter III

Allowances and Benefits

**Red Kabney (Nyie-kel-ma) Allowance**

#### 20.

20. The Red Kabney (Nyie-kel-ma) allowance shall be maintained at the existing rate of Nu.100 per month for the serving Nyie-kel-mas.

**Professional Allowance for Medical & Clinical Staff**

#### 21.

21. Professional Allowance for Medical and Clinical staff will be as follows:

(1) The allowances for General Doctor with MBBS/Dentists with BDS but less than Master Degree and Practicing Drungtshos shall be revised by 45% of the minimum revised basic pay.

(2) The Nurses and Clinical Staff allowance shall be revised ranging from 35% to 55% of the minimum revised basic pay based on number of years served.

(3) The Specialist allowance shall be revised by 55% of the minimum revised basic pay.

(4) A new category of Sub-specialist allowance shall be introduced at 60% of the minimum revised basic pay.

**Radiation Allowance**

#### 22.

22. The radiation allowance of Nu.1,500 per month shall be revised to Nu.3,000 per month and shall be extended to the veterinary technicians exposed to radiation by nature of their profession.

**Uniform Allowance**

#### 23.

23. The uniform allowance for health workers (Nurse) shall be revised from Nu.4,500 per annum to Nu.5,000 per annum.

**Night Duty Allowance**

#### 24.

24. A Night duty allowance shall be introduced at Nu.500 per night for health professionals attending night duty for 12 hours as routine schedule requiring continuous patient care.

**PAY REVISION Act OF BHUTAN 0F BHUTAN, 2019 9 Teaching Allowance**

#### 25.

25. Teaching Allowance shall be as follows:

(1) The teaching allowance shall be revised ranging from 35%-55% on the minimum revised basic pay based on number of years served.

(2) An additional allowance shall be introduced based on Government approved Bhutan Professional Standards for Teachers (BPST) as follows: Proficient Teacher: 10%, Accomplished Teacher: 15% and Distinguished Teacher: 20%.

### Chapter V

Vehicle Import Quota

**Vehicle Import Quota**

#### 50.

50. The Vehicle import quota shall be provided as follows:

(1) A Vehicle Import Quota free of Customs duty & Sales tax of 3000 cc or an option of monetized amount of Nu.1.500 m per term for the Prime Minister, Cabinet Ministers, Speaker, Chairperson of National Council, Opposition Leader, Members of the Parliament and the Chief Justice of Supreme Court;

(2) A Vehicle Import Quota of ceiling Nu.0.800 m; or an option of monetized amount of Nu.0.250 m; or an option of monetized amount of Nu.0.350 m on purchase of electric vehicle for all public servants eligible for vehicle import quota as per existing rules and shall also be extended to the Thrompons and Gups.

50.1 With the introduction of monetized options in lieu of the vehicle import quota, the following terms and conditions shall be enforced:

PAY REVISION Act OF BHUTAN 0F BHUTAN, 2019 19 (1) The quota shall not be transferred/sold under any circumstances and in case it is transferred/sold, the quota holder shall be liable to pay the applicable taxes and duties on the original CIF value of the vehicle and shall not be eligible for future vehicle import quota.

(2) In case of termination/resignation of the Prime Minister, Chief Justice of Supreme Court, Speaker of the National Assembly of Bhutan, Chairperson of the National Council, Leader of the Opposition, Cabinet Ministers and Equivalent Position Holders and Members of the Parliament before the completion of the term, the applicable amount of tax shall be calculated on the original CIF value of the vehicle and shall be recovered on pro-rata basis. However, in case of by-election or transferred in between the term, they shall be entitled for a full one-time vehicle quota free of customs duty & sales tax or Nu. 1.500 million.

(3) For civil servants and other public servants eligible for the monetized amount of Nu.

0.250 million and Nu. 1.500 million, upon death shall be paid the full amount to the next of the kin and on pro-rata basis to the Prime Minister, Chief Justice of Supreme Court, Speaker of the National Assembly of Bhutan, Chairperson of the National Council, Leader of the Opposition, Cabinet Ministers and Equivalent Positions.

(4) The Ministry of Finance in coordination with relevant agencies shall be responsible for strict enforcement.

### Chapter VI

Post Service Benefits

**Pension and Provident Fund Scheme**

#### 51.

51. The Pension and Provident Fund (PF) contribution from the Government shall be revised from 11% to 15%. The additional Government contribution of the 4% shall be contributed into the PF scheme and the total Government contribution shall be 15%, whereas the employee shall continue to contribute 11% only, taking the total contribution to 26%.

The PF scheme shall also be extended to the ESP and GSP, which shall be in the form of Provident Fund.

**PAY REVISION Act OF BHUTAN 0F BHUTAN, 2019 20 Pension Payout**

#### 52.

52. The pension payout shall be revised to 40% of the maximum pay scale of the Cabinet Secretary. Further, the minimum pension payout shall be linked to 40% of revised maximum pay scale of the lowest position in civil service (O4 level). However, an individual has to serve minimum of 20 years to claim pension.

**Gratuity**

#### 53.

53. The existing gratuity ceiling of Nu.1,500,000 shall be removed and revised with no ceiling.

### Chapter VIII

**Royal Bhutan Police (RBP)**

#### 63.

63. The pay, allowances, benefits and other emoluments of RBP shall be revised by the Government at par with the Royal Bhutan Army (RBA) and Royal Body Guard (RBG) to maintain parity amongst the nation’s security forces.
